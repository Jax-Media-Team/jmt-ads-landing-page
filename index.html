Frequently Asked Questions

Everything businesses ask before partnering with us.

How long does SEO take to see results? +

Most clients begin seeing meaningful improvements within 3–4 months, with stronger gains compounding at 6+ months. SEO builds sustainable, lasting rankings — unlike paid ads that stop the moment you pause spending.

What does digital marketing management cost? +

Our management fees start at $1,500/month and most clients invest $2,000–$3,500/month depending on the scope of services. We'll right-size a plan based on your goals, market, and budget during our free discovery conversation.

Do you outsource work overseas? +

No. We are located in Jacksonville, FL and our entire team is US-based. We do everything in-house, white-hat, and by the book. No shortcuts, no grey-hat tactics that could put your site at risk.

Can I start with just one service? +

Absolutely. Many clients start with SEO or PPC and expand as they grow. We'll recommend the right entry point based on your current situation and goals — no pressure to do everything at once.

What does a free website analysis include? +

Upon request, we generate a comprehensive scored website analysis covering 7 categories — technical health, on-page SEO, content, local visibility, backlinks, Google Business Profile, and competitive positioning. You'll receive a full report with a numerical score (0–100), a prioritized action plan, and a 12-month strategy overview. We follow up within 1 business day to walk through the findings.

Do you work with businesses outside Jacksonville? +

Yes — we serve local and national businesses. While we're based in Jacksonville, our SEO, PPC, and digital marketing strategies work for clients across Florida and throughout the U.S.

What's included in your monthly reporting? +

Every client receives a clear monthly report covering rankings, traffic, leads, ad performance, and campaign activity — plus a dedicated monthly strategy call to review results and plan the next 30 days.

Who will I be working with at Jax Media Team? +

The owner and our directors are personally involved in every client engagement. You'll never be handed off to an entry-level person — everyone on our team has decades of experience and we stay actively involved throughout your campaign.
